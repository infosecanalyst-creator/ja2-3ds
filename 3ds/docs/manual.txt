# Controls
== 1. CONTROLS  (1/2): STYLUS
The touch screen is the whole game. The stylus
is the mouse: tap = left click, drag = drag.
For a right click, press B (it clicks at the
cursor, i.e. where you last touched).

In battle, a tap does not act at once. JA2's
touch buttons pop up beside the target:
  Do     confirm the move / shot / action
  Run    move at a run          (moves)
  Item   use the item in hand   (moves)
  Aim    add an aim click       (shots)
  Mode   single / burst fire    (shots)
  X      cancel
You can also steer the cursor with the Circle
Pad and click with A / B.
== 1. CONTROLS  (2/2): BUTTONS
Circle Pad / C    move the cursor
A                 left click at the cursor
B                 right click at the cursor
D-pad             scroll the map
X                 next merc (Space)
                  map screen: pause / resume time
Y                 strategic map  (M)
L                 stance down (crouch / prone)
R                 stance up / climb onto a roof
ZL                swap the screens
ZR                tap: roof level, hold: END TURN
START             Esc: cancel, close windows,
                  map screen: enter the sector
SELECT            this manual
# The two screens
== 2. THE TWO SCREENS
BOTTOM (touch): the complete game. Everything
you click is here; the stylus is the mouse.

TOP: the dashboard. It shows the date, time
and sector, the selected merc's health,
energy, morale, action points and skills,
and the controls for the screen you are on.

SWAP: press ZL at any time to put the game on
the top screen and the dashboard on the touch
screen. Press ZL again (or tap the button at
the foot of the touch screen) to swap back.
The choice is saved. The game then fills the
whole top screen, and the touch screen is a
touchpad: slide to move the pointer, tap to
click where it points (B is still the right
click). The 3D slider is not used.
# Getting started
== 3. GETTING STARTED  (1/2)
The tiny nation of Arulco is ruled by Queen
Deidranna. Her exiled husband Enrico
Chivaldori pays you to free it, town by town.

New game: choose difficulty (Novice is a fair
first try), game style (Realistic, or Sci-Fi
with creatures) and Save Anytime / Iron Man.

You start in the laptop, in your office. The
clock does not run until your first team has
landed, so take your time here.
== 3. GETTING STARTED  (2/2)
First steps:
 1. Read Enrico's e-mail (Mail button).
 2. Hire 3 - 6 mercs on the A.I.M. site, or
    make your own on I.M.P. (chapter 4).
 3. Close the laptop. On the map, your team
    is in the helicopter heading for Omerta.
 4. Press START (Esc) or tap Omerta to land.
 5. In Omerta, find the rebels and speak to
    Fatima, then Dimitri - they give you the
    next lead. Drassen (to the east) has an
    airport: taking it lets new mercs fly in
    closer to the front.
# Hiring mercenaries
== 4. HIRING MERCENARIES  (1/2)
A.I.M. (laptop > A.I.M.): the elite agency.
  Members > tap a face for stats and price.
  Contracts are 1 day, 1 week or 2 weeks;
  longer is cheaper per day. Mercs with
  high Leadership and Wisdom are good early;
  a medic (Medical skill) is essential.
  Some mercs refuse to work with people they
  dislike, or until you prove yourself.
M.E.R.C. (Speck's site) opens a few days in:
  cheaper, paid daily, a mixed bag.
I.M.P.: create your own merc ($3000). A
  personality quiz decides skills and stats.
  Typing a name opens the 3DS keyboard.
== 4. HIRING MERCENARIES  (2/2)
Equipment arrives with the merc (pick "buy
equipment" on A.I.M.). Bobby Ray's (laptop)
sells guns, ammo and armour by mail; orders
are delivered to Drassen airport once you
hold it.

Pay attention to:
  Health / Agility / Dexterity  - survival,
      speed and accuracy.
  Marksmanship                  - hitting.
  Medical, Mechanical,
  Explosives, Leadership        - jobs.
  Experience level              - interrupts.
Morale drops when mercs are hurt, unpaid or
working with people they dislike.
# Tactical combat
== 5. TACTICAL COMBAT  (1/2)
In a sector with no enemies in sight, time
runs in real time. When either side spots
the other, the game switches to TURNS.

ACTION POINTS (AP): each merc gets AP every
turn. Walking, kneeling, opening doors,
reloading and shooting all cost AP. The cost
shows next to the cursor before you click.
Unused AP carry a small bonus into the next
turn.

Hold ZR (D) for half a second to end your
turn. Enemies then move.
INTERRUPTS: a merc who spots an enemy moving
may get to act in the middle of their turn.
== 5. TACTICAL COMBAT  (2/2)
Moving: tap a tile. The path and its AP cost
  show; tap Do (or Run) to go, X to cancel.
STANCE (L / R): standing, crouched, prone.
  Crouched and prone mercs are harder to hit
  and aim better, but cost more AP to move.
Climbing: R next to a low building climbs
  onto the roof; ZR switches the cursor to
  roof level so you can pick roof tiles.
Doors: tap a door, then Do, to open it; with
  the cursor on a door B gives options
  (lockpick, force, explosives, examine).
Healing: bandage bleeding mercs at once
  (first aid kit in hand, then the merc).
  Bleeding mercs lose health every turn.
# Shooting and aiming
== 6. SHOOTING AND AIMING
With a gun in hand, tap an enemy. The chance
to hit shows, with Aim / Mode / Do / X.
  Aim   each tap adds an aim click: costs AP,
        raises the chance to hit
  Mode  single shot or burst (if the gun has
        one). Bursts use more ammo but hit
        more often at short range.
  Do    fire
With buttons: move the cursor onto the enemy,
B adds aim, A fires.

Range and light matter: night, smoke and
distance hurt accuracy. Head shots kill but
are hard; aim at the body.
Grenades: put one in hand, then target a tile.
# The strategic map
== 7. THE STRATEGIC MAP  (1/2)
Y (M) opens the map. Arulco is a grid of
sectors (A1 - P16). Towns are named; red
sectors hold enemy forces you know about.

Moving: tap a squad's name, choose Plot
  Route, tap sectors, then confirm. Squads
  walk; vehicles and the helicopter are
  faster.
Time: X (Space) pauses / resumes. The time
  buttons along the bottom set the speed
  (5 min, 30 min, 60 min, 6 hours per
  second). Time stops automatically when
  something needs you.
== 7. THE STRATEGIC MAP  (2/2)
Entering a sector: move a squad in. If
  enemies are there you choose to attack,
  or retreat when they attack you.
START (Esc) on a highlighted sector with
  your mercs in it shows that sector.

Enemy patrols move too. The Queen will
  counter-attack towns you take, so leave
  defenders (militia, chapter 8).
Airspace: you need SAM sites under your
  control before the helicopter (Skyrider,
  found in Drassen) can fly safely.
# Jobs and militia
== 8. ASSIGNMENTS AND MILITIA
In the map screen, tap a merc's Assign
column to give them a job:
  On duty      part of a squad (default)
  Doctor       heals others in the sector
               (needs a medical kit)
  Patient      rests to heal faster
  Repair       fixes items (toolkit)
  Train Town   trains militia - only in a
               town sector you control,
               costs money
  Practice / Student / Trainer
               raise a stat over time
Mercs need sleep. Tired mercs fight badly.

MILITIA defend towns on their own. Train them
in every sector of a town you want to keep.
# Money and mines
== 9. MONEY, MINES AND TOWNS
Enrico's money runs out quickly. Real income
comes from MINES: take a town's mine and its
head miner starts paying you daily.

LOYALTY: each town's loyalty (shown on the
map) rises as you free it, train militia and
treat people well, and falls if you steal,
kill civilians or lose battles there. Low
loyalty cuts mine income.

Spend on: merc contracts (renew before they
run out!), militia training, Bobby Ray's
orders and medical care.
The financial report (laptop > Finances)
shows daily income and expenses.
# Inventory and items
== 10. INVENTORY AND ITEMS
Tap a merc's face (or the inventory switch on
the panel) to open their inventory. On the
map screen, Enter shows the merc's gear and
the sector's ground items.

Moving items: tap an item to pick it up, tap
  a slot to drop it. Tap a merc's face to
  pass it to them.
Hands: what is in the main hand is used in
  combat. Put ammo on a gun to reload it.
Attachments: tap an item with another item to
  combine or attach (scopes, silencers...).
  Hold (right click) an item for details.
Weight: heavy loads cost AP and energy.
The top screen is the easiest way to read
item names and stats - point at them.
# Saving and options
== 11. SAVING AND OPTIONS
Options screen: from the map screen tap the
"Options" button, or in tactical open the
system menu. It holds Save, Load and Quit.
Use the button below to open it from here.

Saves go to sdmc:/3ds/ja2/SavedGames/.
Save often - before every attack.
Iron Man games only save between battles.

Quitting: Options > Quit, or the HOME button
(the game is then closed).

Settings file: sdmc:/3ds/ja2/ja2.json
Game data:     sdmc:/3ds/ja2/gamedata/Data
# Tips
== 12. TIPS FOR A FIRST CAMPAIGN
- Hire a medic and at least one good shot.
- Fight crouched or prone, behind walls.
- Let enemies come to you; overwatch from
  cover rather than charging.
- Save before entering any sector.
- Take Drassen and its mine early.
- Train militia in every town you hold.
- Renew contracts before they expire - mercs
  leave mid-mission otherwise.
- Night attacks are stronger with good
  equipment, deadlier without it.
- When in doubt, retreat and heal.
